SoluPlay 0.2.2 — the app now plays both HTTP and HTTPS mirrors, and the watch screen gains full screen and a held feed.

The app reads its own catalogue route, so it receives every usable mirror,
including plain HTTP ones the website may not offer. The site itself is
unchanged and stays HTTPS-only.

## Improved

- **Both protocols:** the app's catalogue now carries HTTP and HTTPS links. This
  is the first build that asks for the app's route; builds 3 and older keep the
  browser's HTTPS-only list until they update.
- **Failover:** skipping past the last mirror wraps to the start and tries the
  ones that were live again, one at a time, with the mirror just left tried
  last. A channel that runs out of mirrors has its list read once more before
  the app says nothing is playing.
- **Background audio:** putting the app away pauses every mirror being tried,
  not only the one on screen, and the feed comes back where it was.
- **Watch screen:** full-screen playback, a pause and play control, a loading
  frame around the picture while a mirror is tried, and the channel sheet in
  the band.
- **Home:** the site's seven category pills filter the grid in place.
- **Updates:** the update manifest and APK download accept HTTPS addresses only,
  refuse embedded credentials, and follow only safe redirects.

## Not changed

- The signing key, the application id (`com.soluplay.app`), the Android TV
  launcher, minSdk 21 and targetSdk 34 are unchanged.
