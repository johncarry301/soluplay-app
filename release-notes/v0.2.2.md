SoluPlay 0.2.2 — the phone looks and plays like the site, and the TV becomes a real television: open it, and the first channel is on.

The phone app is rebuilt to the mobile website's own screens, and the TV
app is rebuilt to one picture and the remote. The app reads its own
catalogue route, so it receives every usable mirror, including plain HTTP
ones the website may not offer. The site itself is unchanged.

## Phone

- **Home and watch like the site:** the home grid, the category pills and
  the watch screen follow the mobile website's own layout. The top bar is
  gone; the logo sits in the bottom bar beside the four destinations.
- **Both protocols:** the catalogue carries HTTP and HTTPS links. This is
  the first build that asks for the app's route; builds 3 and older keep
  the browser's HTTPS-only list until they update.
- **Watch screen:** full-screen playback with a swipe to change channel —
  the swipe shows the channel before and after the one playing, so the
  next one is known before it arrives. The controls hide the moment the
  picture plays and return on a tap. The channel list dims after thirty
  quiet seconds and wakes on a touch. The mirror count stands above the
  picture, and the tuning badge always carries the channel's logo.
- **Failover:** the same mirror race and failover as the website — skipping
  past the last mirror wraps to the start, and a channel that runs out of
  mirrors has its list read once more before the app says nothing is
  playing.
- **Background audio:** putting the app away pauses every mirror being
  tried, not only the one on screen, and the feed comes back where it was.

## Television

- **One picture:** opening the app plays the first channel of the list,
  full screen, at once. No guide, no menus, no player controls — the
  remote is the whole interface and the volume keys belong to the TV.
- **A real remote:** up and down change the channel one by one; held down
  they run through the list fast and the picture follows when the key is
  let go. OK opens the channel list over the picture — never beside it,
  so the picture is never compressed — where right marks a favourite.
  Favourites stand first in the list, remembered between runs.
- **Tuning and failure:** a travelling line around the screen while a
  mirror is tried; the static of a television with nothing on it when no
  mirror can play.

## Logos and icon

- Channel logos that the website shows but the app missed now load: the
  loader behaves like a browser (referer, retries, redirects) and draws
  SVG logos into pictures the device can keep.
- A new app icon on both launchers.

## Not changed

- The signing key, the application id (`com.soluplay.app`), the Android TV
  launcher, minSdk 21 and targetSdk 34 are unchanged. One APK carries both
  the phone and the TV launcher, as before.
- The auto-updater: this build is offered to every installed build through
  the same manifest and the same download check.
