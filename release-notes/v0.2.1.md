SoluPlay 0.2.1 — the phone now looks like the website, and the matches carry their flags.

The phone's home, match board, header, bottom bar and watch screen are rebuilt to the
website's own mobile view, and this release carries the fixes that came with them.

## Fixed

- The live dot is on every channel tile again — the site's guide draws it on all of them.
- The match cards are all one height now, so the foot bar no longer jumps from card to card.
- The countdown ticks every second, in tabular digits, so the numbers no longer shuffle sideways.
- Kick-off times are given in Bangladesh time (UTC+6), with the day named where it helps.
- The match board's second column slides every five seconds, the way the website's does.

## Improved

- **Home** is one flat four-column grid in the order the server published, with the seven
  categories as pills that filter it in place. The rail headings and their counts are gone.
- **The match cards** are the site's own card: the title box with the sport's mark, the
  competition line, the two team flags in white 4:3 panels, the red LIVE badge or the
  kick-off time, and the countdown in the foot bar.
- **The team flags** are resolved with the same table the website uses, so the same match
  gets the same flag in the app and on the site.
- **The header and the bottom bar** carry the site's panel colours and proportions. The
  four tabs — Home / Matches / Search / Saved — stay.
- **The watch screen** carries the site's band: the round white logo plate, the channel
  name, the category pill, the LIVE pill, and a channel sheet that opens from the band so
  another channel can be picked without leaving the picture.

## Not changed

- The signing key, the application id (`com.soluplay.app`) and the Android TV launcher are
  the same as in 0.2.0.
- The SoluPlay website, its APIs and the live catalogue are untouched — no site change ships
  with this release.
