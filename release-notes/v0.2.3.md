SoluPlay 0.2.3 — a TV box that opens the app now always lands on the television screen, whatever launcher it runs.

Some boxes do not run the Android TV home screen: their launcher opens the
handset door of the app, and the television was shown the phone — a home
page, a picture with borders, and a remote that could only change the
volume. The handset door now recognises a television and sends it straight
to its own screen before a single frame of the phone is drawn.

## Fixed

- **TV routing:** a device in television mode, carrying the leanback
  feature, or without a touchscreen is sent to the TV screen the moment
  the app opens. The TV screen itself is unchanged from 0.2.2: the first
  channel plays full screen at once, up and down change the channel,
  holding them scans fast, OK opens the channel list over the picture,
  right marks a favourite, and the volume keys belong to the television.

## Not changed

- The phone app is untouched on handsets.
- The signing key, the application id (`com.soluplay.app`), both launchers,
  minSdk 21 and targetSdk 34 are unchanged; one APK still covers both
  surfaces, and the auto-updater offers this build to every installed one.
