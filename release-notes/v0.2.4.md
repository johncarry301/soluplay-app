SoluPlay 0.2.4 — the television now takes the remote's keys: the picture holds focus, so the D-pad changes the channel, OK opens the channel list, RIGHT marks a favourite and BACK closes or leaves.

0.2.3 sent a television to its own screen, but the screen asked for
focus without ever being a place focus could go. Every key therefore
passed the app by, and the only working button on the remote was the
volume. The screen now holds focus itself and reads its keys before
anything else can.

## Fixed

- **The remote on the TV:** up and down change the channel one by one;
  held down they run through the list and the picture follows on release;
  OK, MENU, INFO or GUIDE opens the channel list over the picture; right
  in the list marks a favourite; back closes the list, and back again
  leaves the app. The volume keys remain the television's own.

## Not changed

- The phone app, the TV screen's layout, the signing key, the application
  id (`com.soluplay.app`), both launchers, minSdk 21 and targetSdk 34.
- One APK covers both surfaces, and the auto-updater offers this build to
  every installed one.
